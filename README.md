## Learning Objectives

In this third part of the module, **four practical workshops** will be conducted. Based on notions of human anatomy and physiology, they aim to:

- provide a synthesis of the topics covered in the module;
- give future DS&AI and computer engineers the right sensitivity and knowledge about modern **wearable sensor** and **measurement system** technologies, and their implementation in the most common **biomedical equipment** for measuring biosignals.

Specifically, students will be able to understand:

1. How a measurement is performed in the biomedical field.
2. What problems limit the use of sensors in biomedical measurements.
3. How to select sensors for specific applications in the biomedical field.
4. How to use biomedical instrumentation for the physiological and clinical assessment of a patient.
